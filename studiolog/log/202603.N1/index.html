Afterlives

Location: Steuben Gallery, Pratt Institute Main Campus, Brooklyn, NY, USA

Date: MAY 19 — 24, 2025

Artists: Pratt Interactive Arts alumni



위치: 미국 뉴욕 브루클린, 프랫 인스티튜트 메인 캠퍼스, 스튜번 갤러리

일시: 2025년 5월 19일 — 5월 24일

참여자: 프랫 인터랙티브아트

Playful exists in the choices artists make and the connections those choices invite. In this exhibition, play is both the method and the subject. By treating color, form, and technique as instruments of curiosity, these artists explore humor, motion, and experimentation. While each work follows its own internal logic, they collectively create an interactive environment where ideas meet, collide, and resonate. This approach reveals that creativity is not just about a final result; it is about the process of discovery. It celebrates the willingness to take risks and an openness to unexpected outcomes. As you navigate this collection, we invite you to set your own pace—to zoom into details, uncover surprises, and experience the energy generated when imagination leads. Playful turns the act of viewing into a dialogue. It is a space that celebrates freedom, experimentation, and the joy of discovery in every gesture, object, and moment.

Afterlives는 프랫 인터랙티브 아츠 졸업생 중 두드러진 작업들을 선별해 구성된 기획 전시이다. 디지털과 인터랙티브 미디어를 다양한 방식으로 탐구한 작품들을 통해, 기술과 재료, 경험이 현대 예술을 확장하는 새로운 시선을 보여준다.
