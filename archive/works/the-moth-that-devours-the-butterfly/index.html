The Moth That Devours the Butterfly

2018
Scarf design, hand-drawn
Repeated motifs, approx. 15–20 cm

The Moth That Devours the Butterfly was conceived as an image for a scarf, with its composition arranged to unfold across fabric. Within this field, a moth driven by jealousy begins to consume the butterfly. What starts as admiration slowly shifts into obsession, reflecting how desire toward another can erode the self from within.

The Moth That Devours the Butterfly는 스카프를 전제로 구성된 이미지로, 배경은 패브릭 위에 펼쳐지는 구조를 염두에 두고 배치되었다. 그 안에서 나비를 질투한 나방은 점차 나비를 잠식해가며, 동경에서 시작된 감정이 집착으로 변해 타인을 향한 열망이 결국 자신을 좀먹는 과정을 드러낸다.
