Overview

A kinetic installation that reflects on the quiet persistence of life through a spinning wheel and a robotic moth. The work focuses on repetition, transformation, and the act of continuing.

Concept

At the center of the piece, a mechanical spinning wheel turns in a steady rhythm. When a viewer approaches, its speed and direction shift in an unstable way before slowly returning to its original motion. Above the wheel, a robotic moth moves with a subtle but constant motion, as if driven by instinct.

Stills

Process

The project combines research, 3D modeling, printing, wiring, and motion tests. The spinning wheel and support structure were refined through multiple iterations to create a motion that feels controlled yet unpredictable.
