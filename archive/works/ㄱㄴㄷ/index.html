2024 · Mixed media installation

Still, Life Goes On reflects on the quiet persistence of life amid disruption. The work centers on a spinning wheel that normally rotates in a steady rhythm. When a viewer approaches, its movement becomes unstable—changing speed and direction— before gradually returning to its original state.

This momentary disturbance mirrors how life reacts to external forces. We are shaken, redirected, and altered, yet we continue forward. Nothing remains fixed. Motion resumes.

Above the wheel, a robotic moth moves with subtle regularity. Drawn instinctively toward light, the moth embodies both vulnerability and determination. Its presence suggests an inner drive that persists regardless of outcome.

The work exists between tradition and technology, memory and machinery. It does not offer resolution, but instead lingers in repetition— a reminder that even in stillness, life quietly goes on.
