PublicPixels

A light archive where code, motion, and material leave traces instead of being erased.

PublicPixels is not a single work. It is a field where sketches, patches, sensors, and fabric structures keep updating each other. What looks like a website is actually a log of systems learning to move together.

brand story

PublicPixels started as a refusal to hide test files and half-finished ideas. Instead of cleaning the folder, I treat every failed render and broken sketch as part of the piece.

Coming from textiles and moving into code-based installations, I see each work as a woven system: light, motion, and material threaded together. PublicPixels is the place where these systems stay visible, even when they glitch.

This page is a front door, not a summary. It just marks the tone of the archive: soft grids, small motions, and structures that keep reconfiguring.

Principles

Loose rules that shape how PublicPixels behaves.

01
Openness

Code, process images, and system sketches remain reachable. Nothing is polished out of existence.

02
Motion as language

Movement and repetition describe moods that text cannot. Many pieces in the archive speak through motion first.

03
Human + system

Handcraft, sensors, and algorithms sit on the same level. PublicPixels tracks how these layers negotiate each other.
